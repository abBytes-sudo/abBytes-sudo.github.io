5What not to read into it

Two months, one site, one market. These numbers show a start, not where it will settle.

  • Two months is early. Positions and clicks for new pages keep moving for months, and they can fall as well as rise.
  • Clicks and enquiries are measured, and both are rising. Sales are not claimed here: what an enquiry became is the client’s to say.
  • The enquiry and AI figures are percentages on a small base, from the site’s own tracker. A large percentage of a small number is still a small number.
  • The 28 days before already fall in October, after the work began, so that comparison understates the change since September. The daily-clicks chart shows the longer view, in approximate figures.
  • Indexed pages are pages Google holds, not pages that rank. The jump at the start of October follows the sitemap going in.
  • Average position here leaves out searches for the business by name. Answers from Google’s AI inside search are counted within Google’s own figures.

Questions

The straight answers, including the ones that don’t sell.

Can you guarantee results like these?

No, and nobody honestly can. These figures are what happened on one site in one market, in its first two months. What I can promise is the work: the structure, the content plan, the sitemap, the submission and the checks.

Do you offer monthly SEO?

No. The SEO-ready setup is part of every site I build, and the SEO add-on (setup and content packs) is one-time work. I don’t sell a retainer.

How long before a site shows up?

Pages are usually picked up within days of the sitemap going in. Impressions tend to grow first, and clicks follow. This case covers the first two months, so it shows the early part of that curve and no more.

Will ChatGPT mention my business?

It can, if the site is crawlable, indexed and says clearly who you are and what you do. That gives you a chance, not a promise. There is no trick that makes an assistant cite you.

Does this work outside real estate?

The method does: interiors, construction and service businesses get found the same way, on the questions their own buyers ask. The numbers will differ.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. The same setup applies anywhere Google and Bing are the way people search.
