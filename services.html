How much will it cost?

It depends on the scope, so there’s no rate card here. Send a short brief and you get an estimate and a delivery plan within 48 hours. The estimate lists what is included, what isn’t, and what you pay to third parties directly.

How long will it take, and what slows it down?

Each package shows its typical delivery time. That is working time, counted from the day your content and approvals arrive. Text, photos and sign-off slow a project down more often than the build does. Need it sooner? Ask for an urgent build and the plan will say what’s possible.

Will I own the website, code, domain and accounts?

Yes. Everything is set up in your name, and the code is yours at handover.

Do you do monthly SEO?

No. Every site I build is SEO-ready, and the SEO add-on (the setup and the content packs) is one-time work. I don’t sell a retainer.

Will I show up on Google or ChatGPT?

The site is built so that you can: crawlable, indexed, fast and clear about what you do. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings or AI mentions, and I don’t.

Do I need a custom CRM, or will a spreadsheet or a ready-made tool do?

Often a ready-made tool is the right start, especially for a team under five, and I’ll tell you when that’s the case. Custom earns its place when your process doesn’t fit a rented tool, or when you’re paying for several of them.

Who pays for hosting, WhatsApp and tool fees?

You do, directly to the provider, on your own accounts. They stay yours, and my estimate stays about the work.

How do payments and invoices work? Do you take clients abroad?

Yes, in India and abroad. Estimates are in INR or USD, and payment follows the milestones written into the estimate.

What happens after launch?

Thirty days of support is included. After that a care plan covers updates, backups, monitoring and small changes, if you want one.

Can you work with my existing site or CRM?

Usually, yes. I look at what you have first and say plainly whether it’s better to extend it or replace it.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, on request, before you share anything.
