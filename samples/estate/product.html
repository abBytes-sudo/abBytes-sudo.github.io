Four rules it never forgets. Shown with Amaltas Estates, a 10-person brokerage in South Delhi and Gurugram.

30 / 45 daysRent listings are re-checked every 30 days, sale every 45. One clock drives the Verified badge, matching and the desk.
±10% fitsBudget is a band, the way brokers negotiate. A home 8% over still matches; 15% over is “close”.
15 minutesEvery new lead is assigned round-robin and has a first-call clock.
3 missesA missed call comes back in 2 hours; after three, a person decides. It never unpublishes a listing.

One Tuesday in South Delhi

From a WhatsApp at 10:02 to a token at 6:40 pm.

Twelve leads come in, three agents go out, one family goes from a WhatsApp to a token. The whole day plays in under a minute. Pause anywhere: tap an agent to see their phone at that minute, or a pin to see the lead.

The desk

Twelve jobs a brokerage does before lunch.

Four of them, working, right here: each one is the real screen, with one thing to try. The other eight are a click away.

Try it: press 1 for Connected, pick a next step, then N to save and move to the next call.

The field

The agent’s phone, wired to the office.

Borrowed from how construction teams run sites: today’s work on one screen, big buttons, a photo log, attendance and an end-of-day report. It works with no signal and syncs when the bars come back.
