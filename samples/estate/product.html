Chaukhat runs a brokerage end to end: leads matched to verified homes, calling queues, trips planned on a map, visit outcomes from the agent’s phone, and deals that close with every paper in place.

Shown with Amaltas Estates, a 10-person brokerage in South Delhi and Gurugram.

30 / 45 daysRent listings are re-checked every 30 days, sale every 45. One clock drives the Verified badge, matching and the desk.
±10% fitsBudget is a band, the way brokers negotiate. A home 8% over still matches; 15% over is “close”.
15 minutesEvery new lead is assigned round-robin and has a first-call clock.
3 missesA missed call comes back in 2 hours; after three, a person decides. It never unpublishes a listing.

02

Everything a field brokerage runs on.

Leads, homes, people on the road and the money, in one place that feeds itself.

Leads, matched the moment the brief is in

Portals, the website, WhatsApp, walk-ins and partners land in one list with a source and a “how they found us”. A requirement profile scores every verified home and says why.

Calling queues and call mode

Queues worked out fresh each time, a script per queue, last notes, one-key outcomes and “save and next”.

Verification desk

New and stale listings queue up with photos, a pin check and a checklist. Approve and the Verified badge goes on.

Trips, live

A trip turns “Out now” at the first check-in. Stops, outcomes, photos and the agent’s position update as the field app reports in.

Deals with gates

Token blocks the home. KYC and police verification gate move-in. Commission split and collections tracked per side.

A print issue from verified stock

The monthly magazine is built from listings checked this month; anything stale is flagged before it goes to print.

Map, with demand on it

Listings clustered by colony, where open leads want to live, today’s routes. Drag a box to search an area and shortlist it for a client.

Listing sheets in, safely

Drop a broker’s PDF sheet. The reader understands print shorthand, flags the unsure lines, matches existing listings and shows what it will change before anything does.

Survey master

Coverage by colony, a surveyor leaderboard and a focus mode for owner check calls: last remark first, one-tap outcomes, save and next.

Conversations

WhatsApp and website chat in one inbox. The assistant answers first; take over, use a template, turn the chat into an enquiry with the brief already filled in.

Website and search, first-party

Sessions to enquiries per listing page, what people search for on the site, and Google rankings with a striking-distance list.

Projects, units, cost sheets

Tower grids with holds that release themselves, a price list per project and a cost sheet per unit.

  • Owners — one owner, one call; “update your listing” links and visit feedback on WhatsApp templates.
  • Partners — an independent field-partner network with shared leads and a commission ledger.
  • Reports and admin — funnel by source, leaderboard, freshness, routing rules, templates, SLA timers, export.
  • ⌘K, saved views, bulk actions, undo — and a dark theme for the late shift.
03

The agent’s phone, wired to the office.

Borrowed from how construction teams run sites: today’s work on one screen, big buttons, a photo log, attendance and an end-of-day report. It works with no signal and syncs when the bars come back.

04

Everyone sees their own day.

Roles with a permissions matrix, territories and working hours. One extra permission for one person, without a new role.
