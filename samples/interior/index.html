First, the light.

Before the oak, the linen or the sofa, we find where the sun lands in your room at four o’clock. Everything else is designed around that.

It starts with an afternoon. One of us sits in your room from two until dusk and pencils on the wall where the light goes.

At four it landed here. So the sofa goes here, and the rest of the room is drawn outwards from it.

By half past six it has gone. That is what the one low lamp is for.

What does the first meeting cost?

Nothing at the studio or on a video call. A home visit with the light study is ₹18,000, and it comes off your design fee if you go ahead.

Is there a smallest job you take?

One room, properly. We don’t do single pieces of furniture, and we don’t do a home in under ten weeks.

Do you work outside Bengaluru?

Mysuru regularly, Chennai now and then. Further away, we design and draw; a builder you trust builds.

Will the price move?

Not after the third meeting. If you add something, we price it before we touch it.
