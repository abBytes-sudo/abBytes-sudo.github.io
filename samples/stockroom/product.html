A-03The product

Built like a ledger, used like a phone.

A stock ledger you can audit

Every receipt, transfer, sale and adjustment is a line with a reason, a bin, a running balance and the FIFO cost it consumed. Valuation is never a guess at month end.

Low stock drafts the PO

When an item crosses its reorder point, Stockroom adds it to the supplier’s draft, sized in their buying unit. You approve with one click.

Scan with the phone you have

Code 128 labels are drawn in the browser. The camera reads them with the built-in barcode detector, with a typed fallback everywhere else.

A-04Details

Everything a storekeeper asks for.

Packing slipStorekeeper’s checklist
FromHalden Supply · Bhiwandi
Lines09 / 09
  1. 01

    Items and variants

    Find any chair, lamp or rug by finish, size or colour, with its photo, dimensions and weight beside it.

  2. 02

    Units of measure

    Buy by the carton, sell by the piece. Receipts turn the count into the unit you sell.

  3. 03

    Bins

    See what sits in each of the 36 bins, and move stock from one bin to another.

  4. 04

    Valuation

    Put a value on the shelf at what you paid, oldest cost first, warehouse by warehouse.

  5. 05

    Purchasing

    Approve a draft order in one click, spot the late ones, and book a partial delivery.

  6. 06

    Sales

    Hold stock for an order, back-order what is short, and pick in the order you walk the bins.

  7. 07

    Barcodes

    Print item, bin and order labels, then confirm each pick by scanning with your phone.

  8. 08

    Reports

    Check stock value, slow movers, stock-outs and the sales you could not ship.

  9. 09

    Lumen CRM

    Win a deal and the stock is held for it; the deal sees the new count after every change.
