Stockroom design system

One accent, honest status colours, tabular numbers and hairline structure. Every screen in the app and the product page is built from these tokens and components; they live in stockroom.css.

Open the appProduct page

Colour

Warm paper instead of grey, ink for primary actions, and a single deep-teal accent (distinct from Lumen CRM’s indigo) for selection, focus, progress and the primary chart series. Status colours appear only for stock state, always with a text label. Contrast is measured live against white.

Type

Geist for interface and display at a 14.5 px base, Geist Mono for SKUs, bins and document numbers. Every number that lines up uses tabular figures. There is no display serif: headlines stay in Geist, and the accent is a printed shelf label, never a styled word.

Space, radius and elevation

A 4 px grid. Controls are 6 px round, panels 10 px, the app canvas 12 px. Structure comes from 1 px rules; shadows are kept for things that float (sheets, dialogs, toasts, the bulk bar). Touch targets grow to at least 44 px under 860 px.

Motion

Fast and quiet in the app, slow and expensive on the product page. Never bouncy. Everything has a reduced-motion version.
