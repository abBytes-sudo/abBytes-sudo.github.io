01How one job moves

One job, seven stages.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    An Instagram DM becomes a lead with an owner and a 30-minute first-call SLA. Calls mode logs how it went and books the site visit.

  2. 02

    Design

    Moodboards, finishes with SKUs and 3D renders by room. Meera pins a comment on the image; each room is approved on its own.

  3. 03

    Approvals

    The quote builds from the approved rooms. Ira approves the margin, the client comments on a line, gets v3 and signs on the phone.

  4. 04

    Bids

    Carpentry goes to three vendors. Rates line up against budget, the best one is awarded, and the purchase order writes itself.

  5. 05

    Build

    Milestones by trade with payment stages pinned to them, a daily log from site, snags with photos, and change orders the client signs.

  6. 06

    Handover

    A checklist that has to be finished, the client’s signature, and a warranty register whose clocks start that day.

  7. 07

    Paid

    Each milestone raises a GST invoice. UTR and TDS are recorded, collections mode chases what is late, and the balance reaches zero.

02CRM and design, before a rupee is quoted

From the first call to the signed room.

Two tracks, Turnkey and Virtual Design, each with its own stages, SLAs and task templates that the owner edits in Settings. Every lead keeps one record from the DM to the design fee to the quote.

  1. 01

    Leads by source, with rules

    Architect referrals go to Kabir, Virtual Design to Tanvi, everything else round-robin. A first call is due within 30 minutes.

  2. 02

    Measure once

    The site visit captures room sizes, so floor, wall and ceiling areas feed the brief and the quote quantities.

  3. 03

    Fee before drawings

    The design fee is requested and marked received on the lead, then adjusted against the final quote.

03Work cards, not dashboards

Every screen answers “what do I do next?”

Home is a slim greeting, four tiles that filter, a now-and-next strip, and a stack of cards: approvals, money due, new enquiries, design changes, bids to award, snags. Each card shows the last remark first and has one button that finishes it in place. On a phone it becomes one focus card at a time.

  1. 01

    Context first

    The last thing the client said sits on top of every card, so whoever picks it up next starts informed.

  2. 02

    One tap to log it

    Promised to pay, Paid, Call back, Dispute. The outcome suggests the next follow-up date by itself.

  3. 03

    Safe before it applies

    Change orders and price-list imports show what they will do first: scope, value, milestones, then Apply.

  4. 04

    Gaps filled in place

    No empty cells: “+ Add GSTIN” or “+ Add site contact” right where it is missing.

04Built around how a studio actually runs

The boring parts, done properly.

Every version, and what changed

v1 stays locked the moment it is sent. v3 shows the client exactly where ₹90,000 moved, line by line, room by room.

Approvals with a policy

Margin under 22% or discount over 7.5% gets flagged before Ira approves. Rejections come back with a comment, not a phone call.

✓ Gross margin 27.8% (floor 22%)⚑ Discount 9%, above the 7.5% limit✓ Every line from the rate library⚑ 2 lines use an older rate

GST your CA will like

CGST + SGST inside Haryana, IGST for Delhi and UP sites, SAC codes, round-off, amount in words, FY numbering and an export for filing.

Receivables before they go red

Ageing in five buckets, part payments with UTR and TDS under 194C, reminders that write themselves, and a 12-week cash forecast built from the Gantt.

Handover that has to be finished

Snags closed, keys and drawings handed over, a final walk-through signed. Only then does the client sign, and every warranty starts its clock that day.

Leads you can actually work

Saved views for hot, mine and SLA risk, sortable columns, bulk assign, a board you drag between stages, and every lead opening in a slide-over first.

06On site, on a phone

A daily log in under a minute.

Imran runs three sites from his phone. The daily log is five short steps: site, photos, progress per trade, snags, confirm, with a closing note for the next person. Weather and AQI when GRAP is in force; he never sees a rupee.

  • Snags close only with a note and an after photo
  • Deliveries counted against the PO and challan

07Small things, every day

Fast for the people who live in it.

⌘K

Search and command

Jump to any client, quote, invoice, rate or snag, or run an action, without touching the mouse.

⌘Z

Undo anything

Edit a quantity inline, change a rate, mark a quote lost. Every change can be taken back.

GthenM

Go anywhere, light or dark

Two-key jumps, J and K through lists, C to create. A dark theme for late billing runs; documents stay paper-white.

⌘P

Real documents

Quotes, tax invoices and purchase orders print as clean A4. Every edit lands in the audit log.
