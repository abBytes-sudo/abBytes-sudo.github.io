Race-ready in twelve weeks.

(01) The race

Eight stations. Eight kilometres.

A 1 km run before every station. Weights shown for the Open division; Pro is heavier.

  1. 01

    SkiErg

    1,000 m · Same for everyone

    Long pulls, hips back. Most people go out too fast here because they feel fresh. Hold your 2 km erg pace plus 5 seconds.

  2. 02

    Sled push

    50 m (4 × 12.5 m) · Open: 152 kg / 102 kg incl. sled

    Arms locked, low hips, short fast steps. Never stop: restarting a sled costs more than slowing it.

  3. 03

    Sled pull

    50 m (4 × 12.5 m) · Open: 103 kg / 78 kg incl. sled

    Sit back into the rope, walk it in hand over hand, and keep the rope tidy in the box.

  4. 04

    Burpee broad jumps

    80 m · Same for everyone

    Step back, step up, jump. Breathe on every rep. This is where pacing discipline pays.

  5. 05

    Rowing

    1,000 m · Same for everyone

    Damper 5–6. Use it to bring your heart rate down a notch before the carry.

  6. 06

    Farmers carry

    200 m · Open: 2 × 24 kg / 2 × 16 kg

    Fast, upright, no drops. Chalk at the station and shake out your hands on the run after.

  7. 07

    Sandbag lunges

    100 m · Open: 20 kg / 10 kg

    Knee touches the ground every rep. Find a rhythm of four lunges and one breath.

  8. 08

    Wall balls

    100 reps · Open: 6 kg to 3 m / 4 kg to 2.7 m

    Sets of 25 → 20 → 15… with short planned breaks beats going to failure.

(03) The block

Twelve weeks, written down.

Weeks 01–04

Base

Build the engine and the habit

  • 3 × Heavy Hour
  • 2 × Engine Room (zone 2 heavy)
  • 1 × Station Skills
  • Long easy run, Sunday

Weeks 05–08

Build

Add race-specific work

  • 2 × Heavy Hour
  • 1 × Engine Room intervals
  • 1 × Station Skills
  • 1 × half simulation (week 8)

Weeks 09–11

Sharpen

Race pace under fatigue

  • 2 × strength, lighter
  • 1 × compromised runs
  • 1 × full simulation (week 10)
  • Mobility & Breath

Weeks 12

Taper

Arrive fresh

  • 1 × short strength
  • 2 × openers, 20 min
  • Race-day walkthrough
  • Sleep 8 h, every night
