(02) The method

We don’t sell transformations. We write a week you can repeat for a year.

  1. 01

    Measured

    Every top set and every interval is logged. On the first of the month you get one page: what went up, what stalled, and what changes in the next block.

  2. 02

    Coached

    Most classes are capped at twelve, and simulations at sixteen. There’s one coach for every eight athletes on the floor. Coaches teach; they don’t count reps from the corner.

  3. 03

    Recovered

    A physio screens you in week one. Recovery is a session on your plan, not a suggestion. Members who stay uninjured are the ones still training in year three.

(11) FAQ

Asked at the front desk.

I’ve never lifted. Is this for me?

Yes, and you’ll be in good company: about a third of members start with Barbell Foundations, six weeks in a group of eight. Your trial includes a movement screen, so the coach knows where to start you before you touch a bar.

What is HYROX, exactly?

It’s an indoor fitness race: eight 1 km runs, each followed by a station. The stations are ski erg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, row, farmers carry, sandbag lunges and wall balls. Anyone can enter, solo or in pairs, and it rewards consistent training over talent.

Can I use both floors?

Club and Race Team members can train and book classes at Saket and Gurugram. Open Gym and Off-Peak are tied to your home floor, and you can switch once a quarter.

How does booking and cancelling work?

Book from this page or the app up to 7 days ahead. Cancel up to 2 hours before for free; after that, the spot counts as attended. If a class is full, join the waitlist and we’ll WhatsApp you when a spot opens.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes, for up to 8 weeks a year, for travel, exams, a new baby or an injury, with no fee. Tell the front desk or reply to any of our WhatsApp messages.

Do you have personal training?

Every coach takes a few 1:1 clients. Most members find a monthly check-in (included with Race Team) is enough, with the classes doing the rest. Ask on your trial.

Is there parking?

Saket has basement parking in the building, free for 2 hours. Gurugram is a 6-minute walk from the Sector 54 Rapid Metro station, and there’s visitor parking in Tower B.
