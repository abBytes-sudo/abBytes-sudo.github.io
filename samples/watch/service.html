Ghatika/Care and service

Look after it, and it will outlast you.

A few habits keep a mechanical watch happy for decades. When it needs us, we collect it from your door and send it back regulated, polished and pressure-tested.

Everyday care

Every morning

Wind it, off the wrist

About thirty turns of the crown, clockwise, until you feel it resist. Prahar holds 72 hours; Nimisha and Yantra wind themselves as you move, and like a few turns after a quiet weekend.

Water

Push the crown home

Fine for rain, hand-washing and a swim in the case of Yantra. Never pull the crown when wet, and keep leather straps out of the water: they’ll thank you.

Magnets and knocks

Keep it off the speaker

Phone cases, laptop lids and speakers can magnetise a hairspring and make a watch run fast. If it suddenly gains minutes a day, we demagnetise it for free.

A full service every five to seven years. Not sure? Book a free check in any boutique.

Send it home.

Tell us what’s wrong and how you’d like to send it. We collect from anywhere in India in an insured, tamper-proof box, and send a written quote before any work begins.
