The story

Delivery, returns and payment

Insured delivery anywhere in India is complimentary, hand-delivered by our concierge in Udaipur, Mumbai, New Delhi and Bengaluru. Watches in stock leave the atelier in three days; commissions take about six weeks.

Return an unworn, unengraved watch within 14 days for a full refund. Pay by UPI, card, bank transfer or no-cost EMI over 12 months.

Warranty and service

Five years from purchase, extended to eight when you register your watch. We recommend a full service every five to seven years, with free courier collection from anywhere in India.
