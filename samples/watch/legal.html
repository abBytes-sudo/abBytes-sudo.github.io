Terms of sale

Prices

Prices are in Indian rupees and include GST at 18%. A commission is confirmed when we send you a build date, and the price is fixed from then, whatever happens to gold.

Made to order

Most watches are made after you order. We show the expected date at checkout and update you at each stage. If we are late by more than two weeks, you may cancel for a full refund.

Engraving

Engraving is free and limited to 22 characters. Engraved watches cannot be returned unless faulty.

Shipping

Delivery within India is insured and free, with a signature on arrival and identity check. In Udaipur, Mumbai, New Delhi and Bengaluru, our own concierge delivers by appointment. We do not ship outside India.

Returns

Return an unworn, unengraved watch within 14 days of delivery with its protective film, box and papers. We collect it free and refund the original payment method within 7 working days of inspection.

Warranty

Five years from purchase, eight when registered within 90 days. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship of the movement, case, crystal, dial and hands, and timekeeping outside −2/+4 seconds a day. It does not cover straps, wear, impacts, or water damage after the crown was left open. The warranty passes to the next owner.

Privacy

We use your details only to make, deliver and look after your watch: your order, your appointments, your service history and your warranty. We keep them for as long as the law requires and never sell them. There are no advertising cookies on this site.

  • What we keep: name, contact details, delivery address, watch serials, appointment and service history.
  • Who sees it: our boutique team and watchmakers, and the insured courier for delivery.
  • Your rights: to see, correct or delete your data at any time; write to us and we reply within seven days.

Contact

Book a private viewing, send a service request, or ask the concierge on any page. The atelier is at Haveli No. 14, Lal Ghat Road, Udaipur 313001, open Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 19:30.

Letters from the atelier
