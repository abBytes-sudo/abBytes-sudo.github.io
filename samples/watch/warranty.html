Ghatika/Client care/Warranty

Five years. Or eight.

Every Ghatika is guaranteed for five years from purchase. Register it here within 90 days and we extend that to eight, for the watch’s next owner too.

Engraved on the caseback ring, after “No.”

Buying a Ghatika second-hand? A registered serial shows its warranty, but never the owner’s details.

Anything we made

Movement, case, crystal, dial and hands, against any defect in materials or workmanship. Regulation outside −2/+4 seconds a day is a defect.

Life’s knocks

Straps, scratches, impacts, and water damage after the crown was left open. We’ll still fix it, at cost, and tell you the price before we start.
