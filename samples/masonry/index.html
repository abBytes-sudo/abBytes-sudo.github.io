Case study · No. 01

Brick & glass house

The owners wanted a terrace that read as part of the house, not an addition to it. We sourced clay pavers within one shade of the facade brick, cut the steps from the same stock and laid everything on a drained, compacted base.

Case study · No. 02

The Hollis hearth

A fireplace that anchors a room should be built like a small building. We poured a footing, laid a firebrick lining and clad the chimney breast in fieldstone selected stone by stone, so the colors drift the way a quarry face does.

(08) Process

From first visit to last course.

Five steps, one crew, one person to call. You always know what happens next.

  1. Week 0

    Site visit

    We measure, probe the ground and listen. Free, within 48 hours of your call.

  2. Week 1

    Drawings & fixed quote

    Plans, material samples and a written price with a timeline. No surprises later.

  3. Week 3

    Groundwork

    Excavation, drainage and base. Most of a wall’s life is decided here.

  4. Weeks 4 to 9

    The build

    One dedicated crew, daily clean-up, and a photo update every Friday.

  5. Handover

    Walkthrough & guarantee

    A final walk around the work, a care guide and our ten-year workmanship guarantee.
