Case study · No. 01

Brick & glass house

The owners wanted a terrace that read as part of the house, not an addition to it. We found reclaimed brick within one shade of the facade, cut the steps from the same stock and laid everything on a drained, compacted base.

Town
Beacon
Scope
Terrace, steps, edging
Material
Reclaimed brick, lime mortar
Duration
Six weeks
  • Reclaimed brick
  • Lime mortar
  1. Week 1Survey, drawings and a fixed price. Permit filed with the city.
  2. Week 2Dug out fourteen inches; drain tile run to daylight at the lawn.
  3. Week 3Eight inches of crushed stone in three lifts, each one compacted.
  4. Weeks 4–5Brick laid in herringbone; steps cut from the same stock.
  5. Week 6Joints swept, edging set, a last walk with the owners.
“It looks like it was always there.”
Ellen R., Beacon

Fig. 04 Rounded fieldstone chimney breast on a slate hearth.

Case study · No. 02

The Hollis hearth

A fireplace that anchors a room should be built like a small building. We poured a footing 42 inches down, laid a firebrick lining and clad the chimney breast in fieldstone selected stone by stone, so the colors drift the way a quarry face does.

Town
Cold Spring
Scope
Footing, flue, stone cladding
Material
Fieldstone, slate, firebrick
Duration
Five weeks
  • Fieldstone
  • Slate
  • Firebrick
  1. Week 1Footing poured 42 inches down, below the frost line.
  2. Week 2Firebox lined in firebrick; clay flue liner set.
  3. Weeks 3–4Fieldstone chosen at the yard and laid in lime.
  4. Week 5Slate hearth set, chimney inspected, the first fire.
“The room is built around it now.”
Tom H., Cold Spring

(09) Process

Five steps, one crew, one number to call.

You always know what happens next, and who to call about it.

  1. Week 0

    Site visit

    We measure, probe the ground and listen. Free, within 48 hours of your call.

  2. Week 1

    Drawings & fixed quote

    Plans, material samples and a written price with a timeline. We file the permits.

  3. Week 3

    Groundwork

    Excavation, drainage and a base below the 42-inch frost line. Most of a wall’s life is decided here.

  4. Weeks 4 to 9

    The build

    One dedicated crew, daily clean-up, and a photo update every Friday.

  5. Handover

    Walkthrough & guarantee

    A final walk around the work, a care guide and our ten-year workmanship guarantee.
