AHow we workA-06.1

No surprises after signing.

Most construction disputes start with a scope nobody wrote down. Ours is written down before you pay anything beyond the design fee.

01

Site survey

An engineer and an architect visit the plot with a total station and a soil auger. We measure the setbacks against the bylaws, check whether a ready-mix truck can reach the gate, and photograph the neighbours' walls so nobody argues later.

Time
Week 1, about 3 hours
You pay
Nothing
You get
Survey report
Who
Engineer, architect
02

Concept and estimate

Two concepts, each with plans, a massing model and a cost to within 8%. You choose one, or a mix, and we refine it in up to three rounds.

Time
Weeks 2–4
You pay
Design fee, 40%
You get
2 concepts, 1 estimate
Who
Architect, QS
03

Design and approvals

Working drawings, structural design with a soil report, MEP layouts and the plan-sanction submission. We follow it through the municipal office so you don't have to.

Time
Weeks 5–12
You pay
Design fee, 60%
You get
~70 drawings, sanction
Who
Architect, structural, MEP
04

A fixed-price contract

A 47-page specification, a priced bill of quantities line by line, a programme, and payments tied to stages, not to dates. If something is not in the contract, you don't pay for it unless you sign a change order with its price on it.

Time
One meeting
You pay
10% on signing
You get
Contract, BOQ, programme
Who
Project manager
05

Build

One project manager, one site engineer on site every working day, and 112 quality hold points where work stops until someone signs it off: rebar before every pour, waterproofing before tiling, pressure tests before walls close. Every Friday you get a report with photos from the same camera points.

Time
9 to 14 months
You pay
By stage, 9 milestones
You get
Weekly report, portal
Who
PM, site engineer, crews
06

Snag and handover

We walk the building with you and a 112-point checklist, fix everything on the list, and walk it again. Then the keys, as-built drawings and a manual for every system in the building.

Time
3–4 weeks
You pay
9% at handover
You get
Keys, as-builts, manuals
Who
PM, snagging crew
07

Warranty

Twelve months of defects liability with a 48-hour response, then a ten-year structural warranty. We keep 3% retention until the defects period ends, so the incentive to come back is ours.

Time
10 years
You pay
3% retention, year 1
You get
48-hour response
Who
Service team

BQualityA-06.2

112 hold points. Six of them, in detail.

A hold point is a place where work stops until a named person signs it off, with a photo, in the portal.

  • HP 14

    Rebar before every pour

    Bar sizes, spacing, laps and 40 mm cover checked against the bar-bending schedule by the site engineer.

  • HP 22

    Concrete at the pump

    Slump test on every truck; six cubes per pour, crushed at 7 and 28 days by an independent lab.

  • HP 41

    Waterproofing ponding test

    Every bathroom and terrace holds 50 mm of water for 48 hours before a tile goes down.

  • HP 57

    Plumbing pressure test

    Supply lines held at 1.5 times working pressure for an hour before the walls are closed.

  • HP 63

    Electrical megger test

    Insulation resistance on every circuit, earth-pit resistance under 2 ohms.

  • HP 88

    Window hose test

    Fifteen minutes of water on every opening from outside, checked from inside.
