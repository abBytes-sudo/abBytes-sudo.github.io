Disclaimer

The images, 3D model, walkthroughs and plans on this website are artistic impressions meant to show the design intent. They are not an offer, a contract or a promise. Furniture, fittings and landscaping shown are for illustration.

  • Carpet areas follow the RERA definition: the net usable floor area inside the walls, excluding external walls, service shafts, balconies and terraces.
  • The agreement for sale registered with you is the only document that sets out what you are buying.
  • Travel times are indicative and depend on traffic; the proposed metro station depends on government approvals.
  • The developer may change specifications or plans only as RERA allows, which in most cases needs the consent of two-thirds of allottees.

Prices & offers

All prices are in Indian rupees and "all-inclusive" means basic price, preferential location charges, parking, club membership, interest-free maintenance security and GST at 5%. Stamp duty and registration are paid to the government and are shown separately on the cost sheet.

Prices change by floor and facing and may be revised for homes not yet booked. A price you have been quoted in writing holds for 7 days.

Privacy

What we collect

Your name, phone, email and anything you type into a form, plus which homes you looked at in the unit picker, so the relationship manager does not ask you the same questions twice.

What we do with it

Call or message you about Aaranya, send the documents you asked for, and remind you of a site visit. We never sell it or pass it to brokers.

How long we keep it

Two years from your last conversation with us, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever is earlier. Write to the grievance officer below.

Where it is kept

On this website, forms are saved in your browser's local storage until the sales team picks them up. Clearing site data removes them.

Cookies & storage

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties: no analytics, no chat widget, no ad pixels. Fonts and the 3D engine are served from the same site.

Terms of use

The content on this site belongs to Saptaparni Developers. You may share links and screenshots for personal use. Booking a home happens only through a signed application form and a registered agreement for sale, never through this website alone.

Grievances

If something we said or did is not right, write to the grievance officer at grievance@aaranya.example. We reply within 7 working days. You can also approach the authority directly through its website.
