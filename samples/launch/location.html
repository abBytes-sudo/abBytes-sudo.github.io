Close to everything that matters daily.

On a 24 m sector road off the Dwarka Expressway, between the airport and the city. Pick a place to see the route and the time it takes, at the hour you would actually drive it.

    Times are indicative, from our own drives at those hours, not live traffic. The Sector 109 metro station is proposed, not yet sanctioned.

    Why this side of Gurugram

    The quieter side of the Expressway.

    Roads that are already built

    The Dwarka Expressway and the Northern Peripheral Road are open end to end; the sector road to our gate is complete and lit.

    The airport, without the flight path

    24 minutes to Terminal 3 off-peak, and outside the approach funnel, so there is no aircraft noise over the forest court.

    Schools within 3 km

    Two CBSE and one IB school within 3 km, plus a pre-school planned inside the Canopy Club.
