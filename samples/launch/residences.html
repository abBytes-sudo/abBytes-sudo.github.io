Specifications

What's in every home.

The same specification in every tower and every plan. The show apartment is built to it, with nothing extra.

Structure

RCC frame designed for seismic zone IV, 3.25 m floor to floor, 2.9 m clear ceiling in living rooms.

Windows

Thermally broken aluminium with double-glazed low-e units; full-height sliding wall to the balcony.

Floors

800 × 1600 mm porcelain in living and dining; engineered oak in the main bedroom; anti-skid tiles in baths and balconies.

Kitchen

Quartz counter, stainless-steel sink, provision for hob, chimney, RO and dishwasher; utility balcony with washer point.

Bathrooms

Wall-hung WCs with concealed cisterns, thermostatic mixers, glass shower partitions and a window in every bath.

Comfort

VRV air-conditioning in living and bedrooms, 100% power back-up, piped gas, fibre to the home.

Safety

Five-tier security, video door phone, fire detection and sprinklers in every home, three lifts and a service lift per core.

Green

Designed to IGBC Platinum (pre-certification applied); rainwater harvesting, STP and EV charging in every parking bay.

Handover

Three-year defect liability as required under RERA, a handover snag walk and a home manual for every appliance.
