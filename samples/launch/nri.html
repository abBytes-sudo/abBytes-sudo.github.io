Questions we hear every week

NRI FAQ.

Can I buy without coming to India?
Yes. You can book, sign the agreement and pay entirely from abroad. For the sale-deed registration you either come in person or give a family member a power of attorney; we help draft it.
Which account should I pay from?
Any NRE, NRO or FCNR account, or an inward remittance through normal banking channels. Payments from a foreign account in foreign currency aren't accepted directly; your bank converts them. Each receipt we issue carries the purpose code your bank needs.
Can I take the money back out when I sell?
Generally, up to the amount you paid in foreign exchange can be repatriated, for up to two residential properties, after tax. Your chartered accountant should confirm this for your situation; this page isn't tax advice.
Will TDS be deducted when I sell?
When a non-resident sells, the buyer deducts TDS on the capital gain. A lower-deduction certificate from the tax department can reduce it. The desk can introduce you to a CA who does this every week.
Can I get a home loan from abroad?
Most Indian banks lend to NRIs for up to 80% of the value, repaid from an NRE or NRO account. We'll share the list of banks that have approved the project.
