Amenities·38 of them, on three levels

A club at the edge of a forest.

The Canopy Club is two floors and a roof on the south-west corner of the forest court. Everything else happens under the trees: a 1.1 km shaded loop, a yoga deck, a toddler garden and an amphitheatre for 200.

Site plan

Find it on the plan.

Tap a number or a name. Everything on this plan is inside the gates and included in the club membership.

    A day at Aaranya

    Walk the trail, dawn to the last swim.

    Six stops, one shaded path. Scroll and the light moves with you.

    06:10Forest loop

    1.1 km, all in shade.

    Soft rubber track along the edge of the forest court, eleven benches, a marker every 100 m, and warm low bollards after dark so the trees stay dark.

    07:00Yoga deck

    Under the kadamb tree.

    A raised timber deck for 30 mats, shaded from 7 am by the largest tree on the site, which we moved here rather than cut. Two free classes a day.

    10:30Library lounge

    Work, within earshot of birds.

    Two six-seat work pods and a library lounge on the club's first floor, with fibre, phone booths and a window onto the canopy.

    16:30Play forest

    Mud, sand and a treehouse.

    A fenced toddler garden, a play forest for six to twelve year olds with a timber treehouse, and a half court far enough from bedrooms that nobody minds.

    18:00Club gym

    A gym that looks into the trees.

    2,800 sq ft of strength and cardio, a spin and pilates studio, steam and sauna, run by the club's hospitality partner with trainers on the floor.

    19:30Club roof

    The 25 m pool, heated in winter.

    Six lanes, a shallow family pool beside it and a deck that catches the last of the sun. Heated from November to February.
