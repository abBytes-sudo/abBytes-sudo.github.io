Amenities·38 of them, on three levels

A club on the roof, gardens below.

The Sky Club crowns Tower A with a pool, a lounge and private dining, 155 metres up. At the foot of the towers, the Podium Club and its landscaped decks hold everything else: a 25 m pool, a gym, a 400 m promenade, a yoga lawn and an amphitheatre for 200.

    A day at Aaranya

    06:10 on the podium, 19:30 on the roof.

    Six stops, from the podium at dawn to the roof at sunset. Scroll and the light moves with you.

    06:10Podium promenade

    400 m, car-free, above the street.

    A rubber track around the garden court on the podium, ten benches, a marker every 100 m, and warm low bollards after dark so the towers stay the brightest thing on the skyline.

    07:00Yoga lawn

    Thirty mats, open sky.

    A raised timber deck and an open lawn for 30 mats on the podium, in the first light of the day. Two free classes a day.

    10:30Library lounge

    Work, with the court below.

    Two six-seat work pods and a library lounge on the Podium Club's first floor, with fibre, phone booths and a window onto the garden court.

    16:30Play deck

    Sand, slides and a climbing frame.

    A fenced toddler garden, a play deck for six to twelve year olds with a timber climbing frame, and a half court far enough from bedrooms that nobody minds.

    18:00Club gym

    A gym with a glass wall.

    2,800 sq ft of strength and cardio, a spin and pilates studio, steam and sauna, run by the club's hospitality partner with trainers on the floor.

    19:30Sky Club

    The pool at the top of Tower A.

    A 15 m infinity-edge pool on the roof, a lounge deck behind it, and the whole skyline turning gold. The lounge stays open until midnight.
