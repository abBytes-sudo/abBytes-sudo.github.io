The product

Six screens your team will live in.

  1. 01 · Inbox

    Every enquiry lands in one place.

    WhatsApp, website forms, Meta and Google ads, the chatbot and missed calls. Each lead shows its source, its score and how long it has been waiting.

  2. 02 · Conversations

    WhatsApp and email, one shared inbox.

    Every thread has an owner and a status: open, pending or closed. Reply with a canned template, then log the conversation to the contact or deal in one click, so it shows on their timeline.

  3. 03 · Score and route

    The best lead reaches the right person in seconds.

    Rules you can read add up to a score out of 100. Round-robin skips anyone at capacity, and a 15-minute reply timer turns amber, then red.

  4. 04 · Deals and quotes

    Pipelines that follow your process.

    A pipeline per team, products from a price book with live stock, and quotes with GST, a discount capped by role and a printable PDF. Stage gates stop a deal skipping a step: here, Proposal waits for the site-visit outcome, and the rep sees why.

  5. 05 · Automate

    When this happens, do that. Every time.

    Visual flows assign, create tasks, send WhatsApp templates, update fields and route discounts for approval. Test a flow on a real record first; every run is logged step by step.

  6. 06 · Reports

    Know what this month will close.

    Forecasts by territory and quota, source ROI, a deal funnel and a leaderboard. Build any report yourself and pin it to a dashboard you arrange by dragging.

Marketing and tracking

Every lead tied to the ad or search that brought it.

Lumen records the click, follows the lead through your pipeline and sends the won revenue back to Meta, so your ads learn to find buyers. Search Console queries and campaign attribution sit in the same module.

  1. 01 · Click

    Ad click or search

    A Meta ad adds fbclid; a Google search adds gclid.

    Why it matters: You know which ad, keyword and campaign started every enquiry.

  2. 02 · Website

    The Pixel fires Lead

    Meta Pixel sends Lead from the browser with an event_id.

    Why it matters: Meta hears about the enquiry the moment it happens.

  3. 03 · Lumen CRM

    Lead created, source attached

    utm_*, fbclid and gclid are saved on the record.

    Why it matters: Nobody has to ask “how did you hear about us?”

  4. 04 · Pipeline

    Stages move

    Contacted → Site visit → Quote

    Why it matters: See which campaigns bring leads that progress, not just leads.

  5. 05 · Won

    Deal won

    D-2044 · Terrace Café Co. · ₹6.8 L

    Why it matters: Revenue is tied to the original click, to the rupee.

  6. 06 · Conversions API

    The server sends Purchase

    ₹ value plus SHA-256 hashed email and phone. The server copy of the Lead reuses the Pixel’s event_id, so it counts once.

    Why it matters: Server events survive ad blockers and browser limits, without double counting.

  7. 07 · Back to Meta

    Meta optimises for revenue

    Value-based bidding learns from Purchase value (ROAS).

    Why it matters: Budget moves to people who pay, not the cheapest clicks, and the loop starts again.
