Terms and privacy

These terms cover your use of Ordwell, the white-label work OS, by you, your team and the client guests you invite.

Terms of servicePrivacyData processingSecurityGrievance officer

Terms of service

You may use Ordwell to plan and deliver work for your organisation and its clients. You are responsible for the content you and your guests add, and for keeping sign-in details private. Seats are billed in advance; guests are free.

On the Agency plan you may present Ordwell under your own brand and resell seats to your clients. You must not imply that your clients have a contract with us, and you remain their point of contact for support.

Privacy

We collect the account details you give us (name, work email, company), the work you store, and basic usage data to keep the service running. We do not sell personal data or use your content to train models. Client guests’ data is processed on your instructions.

You can export your whole workspace at any time from Settings → Data, and “Start fresh” there clears it.

Data processing

For customers who process personal data of their own clients, our data processing addendum covers purpose limitation, sub-processors, breach notification within 72 hours and deletion on exit. Indian workspaces are hosted in India in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, single sign-on on Business and Agency plans, role-based permissions, and an audit log of every change. Automations run with the permissions of the person who created them.

Grievance officer

Questions or complaints about personal data go to our grievance officer at privacy@ordwell.app. We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days.
