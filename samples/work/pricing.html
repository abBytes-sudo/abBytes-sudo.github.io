Billing

Money questions.

What counts as a seat?

Anyone on your team who creates or edits work. Client guests who view, comment and approve are free and unlimited.

Can we switch plans mid-year?

Yes. Upgrades are prorated to the day; downgrades take effect at the next renewal so you never lose work you’ve already set up.

Do you issue GST invoices?

Every invoice carries GSTIN, HSN code and place of supply. Pay by UPI, net banking or card; annual plans can pay by bank transfer against a PO.

How does reselling work on the Agency plan?

You set your own price per seat for your clients. We bill you at the Agency rate and you keep the difference. Each client gets their own branded workspace.
