Search help…

Asked by agencies every week.

Can my clients tell it’s Ordwell?

Not unless you want them to. On the Agency plan your domain, logo, colours, email sender name and login page are yours, and the “Powered by” credit can be switched off.

Do guests cost anything?

No. Client guests are free and unlimited on every plan. They see only tasks you tag client-visible, can comment and approve, and never see internal comments or money fields.

Can we resell it to our own clients?

Yes — that’s the Agency plan. You get separate workspaces per client, your own pricing, and a partner margin on every seat you sell.

We bill in rupees and need GST invoices.

Plans are priced in ₹ with GST invoices, UPI and net banking, and annual billing at two months free. Data for Indian workspaces stays in India.
