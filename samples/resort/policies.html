Booking and payment

Every direct rate includes breakfast, Wi-Fi, a 2 pm check-out and GST is shown before you pay. Rates change with the season, the day of the week and how full we are that night; the rate you book is the rate you pay.

Friday and Saturday nights are 15% higher; long weekends and festivals 20%; nights when the resort is more than 75% full, 10%. Stays of 3 nights or more are 10% off, 5 nights or more 15% off. Paying in full when you book takes another 5% off; otherwise 30% secures the booking and the rest is due at check-in.

GST is charged at 18% on rooms above ₹7,500 a night and on bundled meals and services, as the law requires. We issue GST invoices to companies on request.

Date changes are free up to 7 days before arrival, subject to availability and the new dates’ rate. If a road closure or a government advisory stops you reaching us, we move your stay at no charge. Refunds go back to the original payment method in 5 to 7 working days.

Bookings made through travel portals follow the portal’s terms; manage them there.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 2 pm; early check-in from 9 am for ₹3,000 when the cottage is free the night before. Check-out by 11 am, or 2 pm when you book direct. Every adult needs a government photo ID; foreign nationals need a passport and visa, and we submit Form C as the law requires.

Children

Children are welcome everywhere except the Star House, which is for two adults. Under 6 stay and eat free; 6 to 11 pay half on meal plans. Cots and high chairs are free; ask when you book. The ridge has steep edges and open water in the garden tank; children should be with an adult outdoors.

Pets

Dogs stay in Deodar Rooms D1 and D2 for ₹1,500 a night, with a bed, bowls and a towel. They are welcome on the paths and the lawn on a lead, and stay out of the Long Room at meals. There are leopards in these forests; dogs stay indoors after dark.

House rules

Quiet

Quiet hours are 10 pm to 7 am. No loudspeakers outdoors, ever: the birds and the neighbours were here first.

Fire

Bonfires only in the fire pits, lit by our team. Stoves and fireplaces are lit and checked by us; please don’t add wood after midnight.

Smoking and drinking

No smoking indoors or in the forest. Drinks are served at the bar and in your cottage; outside alcohol is fine in your cottage.

The forest

Stay on the marked paths after dark, carry the torch in your cottage, and never feed the langurs.

Accessibility

Deodar Rooms D1 and D2 and the Long Room are step-free from the car park, with grab rails and a roll-in shower in D1. Other cottages have between 6 and 42 stone steps. Tell us what you need and we will plan the stay around it; our team can carry luggage and meals anywhere on the ridge.

Privacy

We collect what we need to host you: names, contact details, ID as required by law, payment confirmation (never full card numbers) and preferences you share. We use it to run your stay and to send the seasonal letter if you ask for it. We don’t sell or share it, except with our payment provider and the authorities where the law requires. You can ask us to delete your data at any time after your stay, except what we must keep for tax records for 8 years.

Terms

Taradhura Hospitality, Dhura village, Nainital district, Uttarakhand. GSTIN 05ABCDE1234F1Z9. Prices are in Indian rupees. By booking you agree to these policies. Disputes are subject to the courts of Nainital. Grievance officer: the general manager, through the front desk; we reply within 48 hours.
