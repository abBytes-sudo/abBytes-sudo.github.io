Taradhura / Weddings & offsites

Take the whole ridge for three days.

Thirteen cottages, one long table and a view nobody will forget. We host about twenty weddings and offsites a year, so each one gets the whole team.

06:20 Venues

Four rooms, one of them outdoors

Full buyout from ₹6.2 lakh a night for 13 cottages and all venues, plus GST.

08:40 How it runs

Two itineraries we have run

Day one · Arrivals and mehendi

  1. 11:00Guests arrive from Kathgodam in our coaches; buransh sherbet at the gate
  2. 15:00Mehendi in the orchard, dhol and folk musicians from Almora
  3. 19:30Welcome dinner in the Long Room, Kumaoni thali

Day two · Haldi and sangeet

  1. 10:00Haldi on the Ridge Deck, marigolds from the valley
  2. 16:30Copper hour photographs on the ridge
  3. 20:00Sangeet on the lawn, under the lights and the Milky Way

Day three · The wedding

  1. 06:20An optional sunrise walk while Trishul turns pink
  2. 15:30Baraat along the orchard path
  3. 17:00Pheras at sunset with the range behind the mandap
  4. 20:00Reception dinner, fire pits, a late night in the library

Guests beyond 26 stay at two partner lodges in Mukteshwar, with our shuttle every 30 minutes. Indicative budget for 60 guests: ₹24–32 lakh including stay, food, décor and music.

Day one · Arrive and think

  1. 12:30Lunch on the deck after the drive from Kathgodam
  2. 14:30Strategy session in the Library: screen, whiteboard wall, fire
  3. 17:15Everybody outside for copper hour (non-negotiable)
  4. 20:00Bonfire and barbecue on the orchard lawn

Day two · Walk and decide

  1. 06:15Dawn birding for the early risers
  2. 08:30Ridge trek to the cliff edge with packed lunch; ideas travel well at walking pace
  3. 16:00Working session, decisions written down
  4. 19:30Kumaoni dinner and the stargazing deck

Indicative budget for 20 guests, two nights, full board with all sessions: ₹8–11 lakh plus GST. GST invoices for the company; 50% advance.
