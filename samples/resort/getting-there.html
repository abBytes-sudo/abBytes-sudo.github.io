Taradhura / Getting here

Closer than it looks.

Most guests take the overnight train from Delhi and are on the deck for breakfast. Here are the three ways up, and what the last nine kilometres are like.

Routes

    Book with a transfer →

    06:20 The last 9 km

    Narrow, tarred, and worth it.

    After Mukteshwar the road follows the ridge east through oak and rhododendron. It is fully tarred and wide enough for two cars in most places; allow 25 minutes. Phone signal drops for about 4 km after the Dhura temple, which is normal. Look for the stone gate with the brass star on the left.

    Winter: snow falls two or three times between late December and February and is usually ploughed by 8 am. We send a 4×4 to Mukteshwar when it isn’t; there is no charge.

    Parking and charging: covered parking for 14 cars at the gate, a 7 kW charger for electric cars (₹350 a session), and a porter with a hand cart for the 80 m walk to the lodge.
