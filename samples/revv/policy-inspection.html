The 150 points

Every car and bike on Revv is inspected by a Revv inspector, not the showroom. The full report is published with the listing, including what failed and how it was fixed.

How the score works

Each point is a pass, an advisory or a fail. A fail costs more than an advisory, and safety items (brakes, tyres, steering, airbags) cost the most. A listing needs 130 or more to go live; anything with an open safety fail does not go live at all.

Fixes are allowed. If a showroom replaces the pads after a fail, the inspector checks the fix and the report shows both the fail and the fix, with the date.

  • Paint depthReadings on every panel; a repaint is named in the report.
  • OdometerChecked against every service stamp we can find.
  • Re-inspectionIf anything changes after listing, the report is updated.
  • Who checksRevv inspectors, who are paid per inspection, not per sale.

What the 150 points do not cover

  • Inside the engine or gearbox: we listen, measure and test, but we do not open them.
  • Problems that only show after long running in heavy traffic.
  • Modifications that are legal but not to your taste. They are listed, not scored.

That is what the 7-day return and the 6-month cover are for.
