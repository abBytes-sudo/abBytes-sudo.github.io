Six months of cover on the expensive parts

Every vehicle sold through Revv comes with six months or 10,000 km of cover, whichever comes first. It is included in the price.

Covered

  • EngineInternal parts, oil pump, turbo, head gasket.
  • Gearbox and clutchGearbox internals; on bikes the clutch basket and primary drive.
  • ElectricalsECU, starter motor, alternator, wiring looms.
  • Cooling and fuelRadiator, water pump, fuel pump and injectors.

Not covered

  • Wear items: tyres, brake pads, clutch plates, chain and sprockets, bulbs, wiper blades.
  • Accident damage, flood damage and anything an insurance policy pays for.
  • Damage from modifications made after delivery, or from racing and track days.

Limits and claims

  • Per claimUp to ₹50,000 for cars and ₹25,000 for bikes.
  • ClaimsTwo claims in the six months.
  • WhereAt the selling showroom or any Revv partner workshop.
  • How fastApproval within one working day of the diagnosis.
  1. Call it inMessage the showroom with the problem and the kilometres.
  2. DiagnosisThe workshop finds the fault and sends the estimate to Revv.
  3. ApprovedWe approve covered work within a working day; you pay only for what is not covered.
